Governing thesis

The case is not an argument against the record. It is the record giving evidence against finality.

This archive reads the clinical and administrative sequence as a matter of ordering. Inquiry must precede finality. Where uncertainty is preserved in the record but certainty is certified in the system, the documents become the witness.

If no one could know, no one could certify as if they knew.
