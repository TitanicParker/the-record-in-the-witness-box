Longitudinal capture

The Drift from iPD Back to iPD

On recurring destabilisation, explanatory capture, and the return of every contradiction to idiopathic Parkinson’s disease.

Not that iPD was never possible. That iPD became the place where every contradiction was sent to disappear.

There is a strange circularity in the neurology record. It begins with a confident drift into idiopathic Parkinson’s disease, wanders repeatedly through facts that should destabilise that label, and then returns to idiopathic Parkinson’s disease as if the journey itself had confirmed the starting point.

The significance is not that iPD was an absurd starting point. It was not. A young man presents with Parkinsonism, bradykinesia, rigidity, gait disturbance, abnormal DAT scan, and response to dopaminergic therapies. A Parkinsonian frame was plainly available. The issue is that the record repeatedly encountered reasons to treat iPD as incomplete, provisional, or possibly wrong in kind, yet each encounter was metabolised back into iPD rather than allowed to govern a new diagnostic sequence.

The record is not static. It keeps thinking. It keeps recording anomalies. It keeps registering the feet. It keeps acknowledging atypicality. It keeps entertaining genetic disease. It keeps observing dystonic or choreiform phenomena. It keeps recognising levodopa sensitivity. It keeps treating pain as oddly responsive to Parkinson’s medication while insisting pain is not directly Parkinson’s. And yet the explanatory centre always returns to idiopathic Parkinson’s disease.

The early record frames the case as Parkinsonism, young-onset Parkinson’s, likely idiopathic Parkinson’s albeit atypical, while also recognising features that strain the ordinary iPD frame: very young onset, possible symptoms for thirteen years, lack of prominent tremor, symmetry, and possible genetic Parkinsonism.

The phrase “autosomal recessive idiopathic Parkinson’s” is almost the whole case in miniature. Autosomal recessive suggests genetic cause; idiopathic means no known cause. The phrase reveals a mind trying to keep the inherited iPD category while admitting that the presentation may belong somewhere else.

From there, the record repeatedly has opportunities to drift away from iPD. Genetics is considered, then postponed. Levodopa is recognised as ordinary treatment, then deferred. The feet return across years as a medication-sensitive focal signal, yet remain symptoms rather than governing evidence. Procyclidine response, Sinemet response, dystonic foot features, severe pain, and patient protest all appear, but none is allowed to reorganise the case.

By the later record, the loop tightens. Pain is said not to be really a direct part of Parkinson’s, while Parkinson’s medication is recognised as relieving the foot experience. Painful dystonia is possible. More Sinemet would probably help further, but the frame remains intact. The dopaminergic system affects the foot pain, but not in a way that changes the explanatory label.

The 2025 rebuttal makes the drift explicit as a defence: whatever the underlying cause of the peripheral neuropathic pain symptomatologies, it remains the clinician’s belief and understanding that the patient has iPD. Parkinson’s magnifies symptoms from other causes. Sinemet helps the experience of pain. Lyrica was used to spare Sinemet. Sinemet CR relief is absorbed back into known Parkinson’s management.

This is iPD as absorptive frame. It can absorb pain by saying Parkinson’s magnifies it. It can absorb Sinemet response by saying Sinemet reduces pain experience without proving causation. It can absorb neuropathy, alcohol, Lyrica, dystonia, foot sensitivity, and medication response. Everything returns to iPD.

The drift from iPD back to iPD is not a story of stable certainty. It is a story of recurring destabilisation followed by reabsorption. Genetic possibility is reabsorbed. Dystonia is reabsorbed. Foot pain is reabsorbed. Levodopa response is reabsorbed. Procyclidine response is reabsorbed. Patient protest is reabsorbed or omitted. Even severe pain with death-wish escalation is reabsorbed.

This is not just diagnostic anchoring in the loose sense. It is explanatory capture.

Once iPD becomes the governing frame, all later facts are required to become either expressions of iPD, complications of iPD, magnifications by iPD, unrelated peripheral symptoms coexisting with iPD, or pain-experience phenomena occurring under iPD. No fact is allowed to become the fact that changes the frame.

A fair expert does not need to say that iPD was impossible. The expert only needs to see that iPD became unfalsifiable in practice. The conclusion explained the evidence, and the evidence was selected or translated to preserve the conclusion.

That is the drift from iPD back to iPD: not one decision, but a habit of record. A way of making every road return to the same place.

Not that iPD was never possible.

That iPD became the place where every contradiction was sent to disappear.
