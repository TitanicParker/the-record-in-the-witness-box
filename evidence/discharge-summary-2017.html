Closure artefact

Discharge Summary — July 2017

The mixed artefact: uncertainty recorded while finality is performed.

The document that was supposed to close the case instead discloses why the case was not closed.

The discharge summary is the central closure document. Its surface declares Parkinson’s Disease as the primary diagnosis. Its body records features that complicate that finality: young age, atypicality, abnormal gait, footdrop-like dystonic posture expressly not foot drop, severe foot pathology, deferred levodopa, possible future regret, genetic possibility, and movement-disorder limitation.

That is why the document matters. It is not empty. It is overloaded. It contains the warning signs and then performs closure over them.

The discharge summary should therefore be read not merely as a clinical narrative, but as a reliance-creating artefact. It closes the admission, fixes the diagnostic frame, transfers responsibility outward, and permits the diagnosis to travel downstream.

Can a document record incompleteness and still safely perform completion?

This artefact should be read alongside The Document That Knew Too Much, The System Did Not Miss the Red Flags. It Neutralised Them., and Finality Before Inquiry.
