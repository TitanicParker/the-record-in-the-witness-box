Reliance artefact

Long Term Illness Scheme Certification — 09 July 2017

The statutory hardening point: the moment a diagnostic hypothesis became administratively portable.

The form is short. Its consequence is not.

The Long Term Illness Scheme application certifies that Liam Moloney is under care for the treatment of Parkinson’s Disease and lists the medications attached to that certified condition. The form is stamped by the Neurology Clinic on 09 July 2017.

Its evidential importance is not that it proves clinical wrongdoing by itself. It proves timing. It shows reliance being created while the broader diagnostic sequence still contained material uncertainty: young age, atypical presentation, unresolved foot-centred symptoms, deferred levodopa, pending DAT scan, genetic possibility, and movement-disorder limitation.

Read with the discharge summary, the form becomes the statutory surface of the same closure mechanism. The clinical record could still speak in qualification; the administrative system required a disease name. The chosen disease name travelled.

The problem is not that the patient received support. The problem is that support arrived as unqualified certainty.

This artefact should be read alongside The Statutory Hardening of a Provisional Hypothesis and Finality Before Inquiry.
