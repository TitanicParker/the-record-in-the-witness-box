Confirmation artefact

DAT Scan Addendum — 13 July 2017

The post-closure confirmatory artefact: evidence after closure, not inquiry before finality.

The scan was evidence. But in the sequence, it was evidence after closure.

The DAT scan addendum records an abnormal scan and frames the result as expected. Its importance is not that the scan is irrelevant. It is not. Its importance is temporal: by the time the addendum was issued, discharge had occurred, Parkinson’s Disease had already been declared as the primary diagnosis, and LTI certification had already created administrative reliance.

The addendum therefore functions as retrospective reinforcement rather than as the point at which diagnostic finality was earned. It confirms an already-moving frame. It does not answer the initiating foot-centred problem, supply a levodopa response, resolve dystonic gait, cure the omitted protest, or make earlier certification properly sequenced.

Support is not sequence.

This artefact should be read alongside Confirmation After Closure and Finality Before Inquiry.
